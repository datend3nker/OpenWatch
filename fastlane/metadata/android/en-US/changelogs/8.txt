OpenWatch now works with OpenVitals on the same phone. The OpenVitals version after 2.16.0 shares your body profile with OpenWatch, shows your watches and scale with battery and last sync, starts a sync, and gets your watch's live heart rate while it records an activity. The new Connected apps screen in Settings shows each app that asked and lets you allow, block or limit it. Health Connect setup now lists the permissions still missing. Nothing leaves the phone.
