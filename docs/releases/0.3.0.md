# OpenWatch 0.3.0

Released 2026-11-10.

OpenWatch now works with other apps on the same phone, starting with OpenVitals. Over OpenLink, a connection that never leaves the phone, the OpenVitals version after 2.16.0 shares the body profile with OpenWatch, shows your watches and scale with their battery and last sync, starts a sync, and receives a watch's live heart rate while it records an activity. The new Connected apps screen shows every app that asked and what it may do, and Health Connect setup now names the permissions still missing.

### Added

- **OpenVitals connects to OpenWatch.** OpenVitals, from Google Play or from GitHub, connects without a prompt and gets what Health Connect cannot carry: the body profile, the paired watches and the scale with battery and last sync, a request to sync one, and a watch's live readings while it records an activity.
- **Connected apps.** Settings, Connected apps lists each app that asked to use OpenWatch, with Allow, Block and Forget and a switch for each thing it may do. An app OpenWatch does not know gets nothing until you answer; a card on the home screen says it wants to connect. Each app's card says whether it is in use now or when it last connected, what its recording is doing, and when its last profile arrived.
- **One body profile for both apps.** Birth year, sex, height and weight are kept the same in OpenWatch and OpenVitals, the newest change winning on either side. The body profile screen says when it is kept in sync and when it last changed.
- **Live readings while another app records.** OpenWatch keeps the watch streaming for the app's recording: heart rate and steps from a Garmin watch, heart rate from a Wear OS watch. A silent notification, "Streaming heart rate to OpenVitals", is up for as long as it lasts.
- **Health Connect setup lists what is missing.** The permissions not granted yet are named as Android names them, with where background and history access are granted.

### Changed

- **Privacy policy.** A new section, "Apps You Connect On Your Phone", covers what a connected app can receive. The list of what each device writes to Health Connect now matches the records exactly, and the policy names the publisher, MTÜ Hale, with hello@hale.ngo as its contact.
- **Languages.** The new status lines are translated in all thirteen languages. The titles and buttons of Connected apps, and the home, Health Connect setup, body profile and settings screens, are still English in every language.

The phone app and the Wear OS app share the package name `tech.mmarca.openwatch` and the signing certificate, so both install as a normal update; the watch's versionCode is 2000000000 plus the phone's.

Distribution flow:

- GitHub release with the signed phone APK, signed debug APK, signed Wear OS APK and both signed Android App Bundle assets.
- Direct Google Play production upload, to the phone track and the Wear OS track, from the approved production run of the release workflow.
